Privacy Policy - Hatch Whoop Connector

Last Updated: August 17, 2026 - Personal Development Version - Not Legal Advice

This is a personal development integration that connects your Whoop account to your Hatch personal AI workspace. This policy describes what data is accessed and how it is handled.

Data We Access

When you connect Whoop, this app requests these Whoop OAuth scopes:

  • read:recovery - recovery scores, HRV, resting heart rate
  • read:cycles - daily strain, cycle data
  • read:workout - workout history
  • read:sleep - sleep duration, stages
  • read:profile - basic profile (name, email)
  • read:body_measurement - height, weight, max HR
  • offline - allows refresh token so you don't have to log in every time

How We Use It

Data is used only to show your own Whoop metrics inside Hatch (your private workspace) for personal wellness tracking, to support your weight-loss / fitness goals. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other service.

Storage

Data is fetched via the official Whoop API at api.prod.whoop.com and stored only in your local Hatch workspace / local environment. No external database or third-party analytics.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your Whoop data with third parties. No data is shared with anyone other than you.

Deletion & Revoke

You can revoke access anytime:

  1. In Whoop app: Settings > App Settings > Manage Connected Apps > Revoke
  2. In Hatch: Disconnect the custom.whoop connector
  3. To delete local cached data: delete the files in your workspace skills storage.

Upon revocation, refresh and access tokens are invalidated and no new data is fetched.

Security

OAuth tokens are stored via Hatch's auth system (authd) and never hard-coded or committed to GitHub. All API calls are over HTTPS.
